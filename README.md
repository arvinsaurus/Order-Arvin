## What is this?

Order Arvin is a colour-sorting puzzle game where you sort liquids into bottles until each bottle holds only one colour. Simple in concept, deeply satisfying to play.

Built from scratch, by hand, with love — by a husband who just wanted his wife to have a better experience.

## Features

- 🎮 Multiple levels of increasing challenge
- ↩️ Undo moves when things go sideways
- 🔊 Sound toggle for peaceful playing
- 📱 Fully mobile-friendly — play on your phone, Kirana!
- No ads. Ever.
